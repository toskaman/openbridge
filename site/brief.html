OpenBridge
Partner brief, November 2026

Give your hardware powers it never shipped with.

OpenBridge connects the devices people already own and lets an AI build brand-new controls across them, on demand. Every action is approved by the owner and checked on the real device afterwards.

What OpenBridge is

A local-first layer that discovers the devices in a home or a company, learns what each one can really do, and composes new controls between them: vacuum buttons on a TV, a TV remote on a watch, alerts in smart glasses. The AI runs on the owner's machine. Nothing runs without approval, and “done” is only reported after the device is read back.

How a partnership works

  1. You send a device to keep and its API or SDK documentation.
  2. We build the adapter and an emulator of your product, so work starts at once.
  3. Tests and benchmarks run on the real unit, on every release, in CI on three operating systems.
  4. You get a verified compatibility report and your brand featured as a hardware partner.

What we ask

Hardware to keep: donated units or a permanent loan, never a time-limited one, so tests keep running for the life of the integration. Plus API / SDK access and a technical contact.

What your company gains

  • New features, without new firmware. Controls, alerts, voice and cross-device flows on top of what your device already exposes.
  • Interoperability with TVs, watches, phones, routers, robots and PCs from other brands.
  • Complete test suites and benchmarks written for your product and run on the real hardware.
  • Wider compatibility: USB, Wi-Fi, BLE or relay, on 6 platforms, even legacy Windows.
  • An emulator of your device for your developers, QA and sales demos.
  • Expert API / SDK feedback and a public, verified compatibility report.
