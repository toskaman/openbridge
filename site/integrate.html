Become a partner

Bring your device, leave with an integration.

Drop your device's OpenAPI description. You get an OpenBridge adapter (typed settings with read-back and exact Undo, commands, refusals), an emulator of your device and tests that pass on day one. Generated here, in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

1. Your API description

Drop an OpenAPI 3 file (JSON) here, or click to choose one

The rules are fixed and published: a bounded field the device also reports becomes a setting (read back, exact Undo); a POST with no body or a field it does not report becomes a command; anything about firmware, updates, resets, unlocking or passwords is always refused; the rest is listed with the reason. Same result as openbridge partner new NAME --from-openapi FILE.
