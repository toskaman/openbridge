## Services, on their own or added to a plan

| Service | What you get |
|---|---|
| **[API and SDK audit](AUDIT.md)** | A written integrator's report: protocol quirks, documentation gaps, firmware pitfalls, reproducible test cases. The ENGO dossier (19 documents) is an example. |
| **[Voice model for your product](../PARTNER_VOICE_MODEL.md)** | OpenBridge's command model trained on your product names, commands and languages; runs offline; scored on requests nobody trained on. |
| **[Skill pack](../SKILL_PACKS.md)** | Ready-made features showing your device working with other brands, installable in one command, reviewed and pinned by checksum. |
| **[Neutral benchmark run](BENCHMARK.md)** | Your device measured with the public method (latency, reliability, exact Undo, refusals). Results are published as measured. |
| **[Co-marketing](CO_MARKETING.md)** | An animation and a demo video with your device, a case study, a joint demo at a trade show. |
| **Try it in 5 minutes** | [Bring your device, leave with an integration](https://toskaman.github.io/openbridge/integrate.html): an adapter, an emulator and passing tests generated in your browser from your API description ([how it works](../INTEGRATE_YOUR_DEVICE.md#from-an-api-description)). |
