# OpenBridge partner licence: term sheet

This is the outline of the written licence offered to hardware partners. It is a basis for discussion, not a licence
by itself: the binding terms are those of the agreement both parties sign. It should be reviewed by counsel before
signature. [Version française plus bas](#version-française).

Before an agreement, a company can test OpenBridge for free for 90 days under the
[evaluation licence](../../LICENSES/OpenBridge-Evaluation-1.0.md). Plans side by side: [Plans](PLANS.md).

## 1. Parties and scope

- **Licensor:** the OpenBridge author (contact: info@lepcpro.fr).
- **Licensee:** the partner company.
- **Licensed software:** the OpenBridge core, at the versions released during the term, as described in
  [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) (everything except the Apache-2.0 integration kit, which needs no licence).
- **Integration:** the adapter, emulator, graph entry, tests and benchmarks for the partner's named products.

## 2. Rights granted to the partner

Non-exclusive, worldwide, for the term, for the integration of the named products:

1. **Use** the core internally, commercially, for development, QA and continuous integration.
2. **Demonstrate** the core with the integration to customers, distributors and press.
3. **Modify** the core for testing and interoperability fixes, and submit those changes to OpenBridge.
4. **Distribute** the core, unmodified or with the agreed changes, inside the partner's own products or apps
   (only if this option is selected in the agreement; it may carry a fee or a revenue share).

## 3. What stays with each party

- The partner's firmware, SDKs, documentation, test data, trademarks and own code remain the partner's. OpenBridge
  receives only the rights needed to build and test that partner's integration.
- The OpenBridge core, name and logo remain the licensor's. No trademark rights unless agreed in writing
  (for example "Works with OpenBridge").
- Integration code generated by the kit is Apache 2.0 in any case; code written by the partner is licensed as the
  partner chooses.

## 4. Restrictions

- No sublicensing, sale or distribution of the core outside the partner's products.
- No removal of licence notices.
- Safety policies (consent, read-back, refusal of doors, garage doors, gates and alarms) are not removed from
  distributed builds.

## 5. Confidentiality, support, term

- Mutual NDA available; confidential material stays out of the public repository.
- Support level, response times and evidence reports (benchmarks, device evidence) as agreed.
- Term set in the agreement (proposed: 12 months, renewable). On termination, internal use stops; units already
  shipped keep their licence.
- Hardware lent or donated to OpenBridge: donated units or a permanent loan, never a time-limited one.

## 6. Fees

Set in the agreement. Distribution rights (2.4) are priced case by case.

## Version française

Résumé de la licence partenaire. Ce n'est pas une licence en soi : les conditions qui engagent sont celles de
l'accord signé par les deux parties, à faire relire par un juriste.

- **Ce qui est libre sans accord :** le kit d'intégration (`src/partner_kit.py`, les fichiers générés par
  `openbridge partner new`, le guide d'intégration) est sous **Apache 2.0**. Votre propre code reste sous la licence
  de votre choix (propriétaire, MIT, Apache...).
- **Ce que la licence partenaire ajoute :** utiliser le cœur d'OpenBridge (sous PolyForm Strict) commercialement en
  interne, en QA et en CI ; le montrer à vos clients ; le modifier pour l'interopérabilité ; et, si l'option est
  retenue, le distribuer dans vos produits ou applis.
- **Ce qui reste à chacun :** vos firmwares, SDK, docs, données de test et marques restent à vous ; le cœur, le nom
  et le logo OpenBridge restent à l'auteur d'OpenBridge.
- **Interdits :** sous-licencier ou vendre le cœur hors de vos produits, retirer les mentions de licence ou les
  protections de sécurité.
- **Confidentialité :** NDA possible ; rien de confidentiel n'est publié.
- **Durée et prix :** fixés dans l'accord (proposition : 12 mois renouvelables). Matériel : don ou prêt permanent, jamais un prêt à durée limitée.
