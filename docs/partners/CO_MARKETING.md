# Co-marketing

What OpenBridge and a partner can show together, and the one rule behind it: **every claim comes from evidence**.
A demo that runs on an emulator, or a feature that is not shipped yet, is labelled as such on screen.

| What | Description |
|---|---|
| **Animation** | An animated scene with your device next to other brands, like the one on the [README](../../README.md) (SVG, plays on GitHub and on the web, 10 to 20 seconds). |
| **Demo video** | Filmed on the real hardware, in a real room, with the receipts on screen, like the [project demo](https://toskaman.github.io/openbridge/#demo). One to three minutes. |
| **Case study** | One page: the problem, the integration, the measured results (benchmark, evidence runs), a quote from your team. Published here and yours to reuse. |
| **Trade-show demo** | A kit that runs offline on a laptop: your device, two or three other brands, the skill pack and the HUD or TV view. Staffed on request. |
| **Listing** | Your device on the [compatibility page](https://toskaman.github.io/openbridge/compatibility.html), with its level (real hardware, emulator) and, when earned, the [Verified](VERIFIED.md) label. |
| **Skill pack** | A [pack](../SKILL_PACKS.md) showcasing your device with others, installable in one command. |

Each piece is reviewed by both sides before it is published. Logos and trademarks are used only as each owner allows.
On a partner plan, one animation, one case study and the listing are included; videos and shows as agreed
(see [Plans](PLANS.md)).
